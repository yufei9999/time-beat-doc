# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

## 1. Introduction

Thank you for using this application (the “App”). We highly value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your personal information when you use the App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 1) Gameplay Performance Data

- Deviation time: the difference between the time you tap the stop button and the target time (9.9 seconds) for each game, measured to four decimal places in seconds, including both positive and negative values.
- Game timestamps: the completion time recorded for each game.
- Rating information: ratings generated based on your deviation (Perfect / Master / Expert / Good / Miss) and whether you hit the perfect deviation threshold (Perfect Hit).

### 2) Device Information

- Device model (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro): used to analyze performance across different devices.
- Operating system version (e.g., iOS 18.0): used for compatibility analysis and usage statistics.
- Unique device identifier: a system-generated UUID stored locally, used to identify the same device across game sessions. It is not uploaded to the server unless you choose to submit a score to the leaderboard.

### 3) Information You Provide Voluntarily

- Nickname: optional, which you may fill in when submitting a score to the leaderboard. If left empty, the system will display “Anonymous.” Your nickname is submitted together with the score data to the leaderboard server.

### 4) App Preferences (Stored Locally Only)

- Sound effect toggle status
- Haptic feedback toggle status
- Language preference
- First launch date

These settings are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to any server.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

1. Core functionality: calculate and display your timing deviation and provide gameplay rating feedback.
2. Local data storage: save your historical game results on your device using Core Data.
3. Leaderboard service: with your consent, submit game results (deviation, rating, nickname, device model, system version) to the cloud leaderboard server for you and others to reference.
4. Device identification: use the local UUID as your identity marker to distinguish records across devices.
5. Product improvement: analyze anonymized statistics to optimize the user experience.

## 4. Audio Function Statement

This App uses the following audio-related technologies:

| Technology | Purpose | Microphone permission required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AVAudioSession | Configure the audio session and allow coexistence with other audio apps (mixing mode) | No |
| AVAudioEngine / AVAudioPlayerNode | Play in-app sound effects (countdown, heartbeat, stop, perfect hit, failure prompts) | No |
| CoreHaptics | Provide haptic feedback (vibration) | No |

Important notice: This App does not use the microphone and does not collect any audio input data. All audio is played from in-app sound files, and no microphone authorization is required.

In the App Store permission declaration, the audio permission declared by this App is limited to system audio session management and does not involve microphone capture. The app only plays built-in sound effects and does not access or record the user’s audio input.

## 5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We will not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party.

The only exception is that when you choose to submit a score to the leaderboard, your game data will be transmitted to our cloud server (Supabase). The server provider complies with applicable data protection regulations, and we have taken reasonable measures to ensure the security of your data.

## 6. Data Storage and Retention

- Local data: your game history is stored permanently on your device. You can clear your history through the App settings.
- Cloud data: leaderboard scores are stored on the Supabase server and retained based on a daily submission limit. Scores exceeding the limit will not be submitted, but this does not affect your local records.
- Data cleanup: when the number of local records exceeds 1,000, the system automatically deletes the oldest records to free up storage space.

## 7. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the following rights:

1. Right of access: view your personal data generated in the App at any time.
2. Right to rectification: correct inaccurate information (such as a nickname).
3. Right to erasure: request deletion of your game score records.
4. Right to withdraw consent: you may choose not to submit scores to the leaderboard at any time, and this will not affect your continued use of the App’s core functions.
5. Right to object: if you believe that our handling of personal information is inappropriate, you may object to us.

To exercise the above rights, please contact us at yufei.cjn@outlook.com.

## 8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including:

- Using HTTPS to encrypt all network transmission
- Implementing row-level security (RLS) on the server to restrict data access
- Encrypting local data storage

However, we cannot guarantee absolute security for internet transmission. Please keep your personal information secure.

## 9. Protection of Minors

This App is intended for users of all ages. For minor users, we recommend that they use the App under the guidance of a guardian. We do not intentionally collect personal information from minors. If we discover any collection of minor information without parental consent, we will delete it as soon as possible.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated policies will be announced in the App or communicated to you in other appropriate ways. We recommend that you review this policy periodically to understand how we protect your information.
